## Style Prompt

Memento Mori Jester is a dark, terminal-native safety tool with a court-jester edge: sharp, useful, a little irreverent, and never fluffy. The video should feel like a serious developer tool being interrupted by a witty conscience. Use a vertical social-video layout with readable terminal panels, crisp annotation bubbles, and restrained theatrical accents.

## Colors

- Background: `#11100e`, warm near-black
- Surface: `#1b1814`, terminal panel
- Foreground: `#f2eadc`, bone text
- Muted: `#b7a990`, secondary text
- Red accent: `#d64b3f`, block / warning
- Gold accent: `#d7a642`, jester / callout
- Green accent: `#69b27c`, pass / healthy

## Typography

- Display: `"Georgia"`, serif, heavy for product name and big claims
- Mono/body: `"IBM Plex Mono"`, monospace, for terminal output, labels, and bubbles

## What NOT To Do

- No neon cyberpunk palette.
- No generic blue-purple gradients.
- No tiny terminal type.
- No marketing landing-page hero layout.
- No fake product UI; show real command behavior.

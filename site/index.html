Memento Mori Jester

A local court-jester sidecar for AI coding agents. It reviews commands, plans, diffs, and final answers before the agent gets too pleased with itself.

What it reviews

The jester catches the obvious dangerous move, then keeps going: plans without checks, risky diffs, and final answers that claim victory without evidence.

Commands

Blocks destructive shell moves such as git reset --hard before they become a bad afternoon.

Plans

Cautions on overconfidence, missing verification, and high-risk areas such as auth or production.

Diffs

Reviews package scripts, secrets, sensitive env changes, removed tests, and broad risky domains.

Final answers

Calls out done/fixed/works claims that do not include the evidence a human needs.
